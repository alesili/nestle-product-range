# Brand inclusion rules

Included if: (1) any brand tag starts with `nestle`, or (2) a tag in sql/brands.txt.

- Group 1 (in brands.txt): brands owned by Nestle worldwide with unambiguous names.
- Group 2 (only when also tagged `nestle`): kitkat, kit-kat, smarties, quality-street,
  cheerios, trix, carnation, fitness, chocapic. Ownership depends on the country.
- Excluded: nan, aero (names too ambiguous); perrier, san-pellegrino, vittel
  (ownership of the water business to be verified); orion, familia, froneri, gloria
  (other companies or joint ventures; included only when tagged `nestle`).
- Pet food (for example Purina) is not in this database.
